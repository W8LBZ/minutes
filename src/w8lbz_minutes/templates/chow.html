Fill the schedule

Start with someone else

Every open meeting night gets the next member in order, going around the list as many times as needed; holidays are filled like any other night. Nights already marked "No chow" (say, after the club votes on a holiday) are skipped. Someone who can't do a night (see their limits below) keeps their turn and gets the next night they can do.

Rotation

The same order every round. New members join at the end of the current round (just before whoever is next), so everyone already on the list goes first.
